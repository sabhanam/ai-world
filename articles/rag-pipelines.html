RAG (retrieval-augmented generation)

A way to make an AI answer from your own documents by looking up the relevant bits first and handing them to the model.

First found on en.wikipedia.org. Researched from 1 source.

In plain words

An LLMlarge language model, the kind of AI behind chat assistants, trained on huge amounts of text to predict and write language. can't read all your company's documents at once, and it can't know what it was never trained on. RAG fixes this by searching your documents for the few passages that matter to a question, then giving those passages to the model along with the question. The model answers using them, so its answer is grounded in your material.
